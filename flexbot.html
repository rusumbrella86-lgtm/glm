YOUR PRIVATE CHANNEL SETTINGS

Your welcome messages 0 / 10

New channels start with 10 welcoming greetings. Edit, replace, disable, or delete any message to make it your own. Use {user} for the viewer’s name and {channel} for your channel.

Personal greetings 0 / 10

Give specific people their own welcome using @username. A matching personal greeting replaces their general welcome, once per day in your channel. Matching ignores letter case and a leading @.

Your custom commands 0 / 10

Choose a command and the text Flexbot replies with. Everyone in your chat can use enabled custom commands. Built-in commands keep the admin’s permissions and cannot be replaced here.

Use {user}, {channel}, or {query} in a reply. Command names use 1–30 letters, numbers, underscores, or hyphens after !.

LM Studio replies

Give the bot a voice that fits your channel. Choose up to 10 reply commands, a tone, and instructions. Flexbot uses the admin’s connected model, so you don’t need to install LM Studio yourself. These preferences apply only in your chat.

LM Studio chat commands 1 / 10

Viewers type any of your commands followed by a question. All commands share your reply settings and cooldown. Cooldowns are 10–3600 seconds per viewer. Regulars are managed by the admin.

Reply to specific usernames 0 / 20

No command needed: reply to a listed viewer’s new messages in your chat. Each person can have a different style, cooldown, and extra instructions. Matching ignores letter case and a leading @.

Replies start after you enable this option and save. Your signup must be active and the bot’s model online. The admin’s LM Studio switch, blocked words, and Blocked Users always apply. Automatic username replies also need Personal messages enabled for your channel. Your preferences stay saved after expiry.

All changes saved

Saved changes apply when Flexbot next syncs your active channel. Channel permissions, cooldowns, blocked words, and Blocked Users still apply.

Verified channel owners can now customize LM Studio replies alongside their greetings and commands. Open Manage my channel, choose LM Studio replies, and save your preferences.

LM STUDIO REPLIES

Choose your bot’s personality

Turn AI replies on or off, choose a command, set who can ask, and pick a style from helpful to sarcastic or a savage roast. Add your own channel instructions and per-viewer cooldown.

AUTOMATIC USERNAME REPLIES

A different style for each person

Add up to 20 usernames with individual styles, instructions, and cooldowns. They only apply in your channel. The admin runs the model and keeps blocked words and channel permissions in effect.

WELCOME MESSAGES

Up to 10 greetings

Write your own welcome messages. Pick one specific greeting or let Flexbot randomly choose from the greetings you enable.

PERSONAL GREETINGS

A hello for @username

Save up to 10 personal greetings for specific people. Their personal message replaces the general welcome, once per day in your channel.

CUSTOM COMMANDS

Up to 10 commands

Create commands such as !socials, write the replies, and set their cooldowns. You can enable or disable each command. Existing built-in commands keep their admin settings.

SAVED FOR YOUR RETURN

Renew without starting over

Choose 1–7 days when signing up. Bot access ends when that period expires, but your greetings and commands stay saved for your next signup unless the admin clears your expired signup and settings.

How to get started

New here? Choose Sign up or renew and verify your channel. Already signed up? Choose Manage my channel to open your private settings, including after expiry. Managing settings does not extend your signup.

Save your changes with Save my settings. Flexbot uses them after its next sync and joins your live chat during your active signup while the bot service is online. Channel permissions and Blocked Users still apply.
